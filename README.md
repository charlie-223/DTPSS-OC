# One Cainta — Disaster Preparedness Training and Simulation

One Cainta opens with a public information landing page describing the platform and its five operational modules, with direct links to official Municipality of Cainta updates. Users, Staff, and Admins use **Sign In** to reach their secured, role-specific workspace, which shares the home page's Cainta masthead, official updates bar, and visual styling across all roles. The workspace and its mobile navigation are shown only after the account role has been verified. Self-registered accounts receive User portal access automatically; Staff and Admin roles require an authorized invitation or role assignment. The system has three active roles:

- **User** gets a separate community portal with **My Dashboard**, **My Sessions**, **My Performance**, **Browse Drills**, **Household Preparedness**, and **My Certificates**. A notifications bell beside the theme control shows relevant training and simulation announcements. Users can enroll in training sessions, cancel their own enrollment, review only their own evaluation feedback, browse preparedness drills, and manage their own contact details. User accounts cannot create or edit shared programs, scenarios, or reports, and cannot manage accounts.
- **Staff** represents instructors. Staff see and update only training programs assigned to their account; their workspace lists programs by disaster type, including Flood, Earthquake, Fire, and Typhoon. Staff cannot create or delete programs, change instructor assignments, access other workspace modules, or manage accounts.
- **Admin** gets the full operations dashboard and account-management tools, including **Training Program Management**, **Simulation Scenario Builder**, **Participant Registration**, **Performance Evaluation**, and **Disaster Drill Reporting**. Admins can invite users and staff, assign roles, and assign instructors to training programs. The account page shows each account's full name, email, role, Active/Inactive display status, and recent workspace activity. Admin accounts cannot be deleted directly, and the database prevents demoting the last Admin. Deletion removes the Auth account and its profile and cannot be undone. Account status does not change sign-in access. Passwords are never shown. A visible authenticated workspace records activity every 30 seconds; “Active now” means activity was recorded within the last 45 seconds, and older activity is shown in seconds, minutes, hours, days, months, or years.

Role checks are enforced by PostgreSQL row-level security and the workspace synchronization function, not just by hiding interface controls. Users can browse public training and drills and manage only their own enrollments and notifications; they can read only their own participant and evaluation records. Staff can read and update only programs assigned to their instructor account. Admins can manage workspace records and accounts. Notifications are role-scoped: Residents receive training and simulation updates, Staff receive updates for assigned programs/scenarios, and Admins receive program/scenario notices plus Staff field reports and resident evaluations. All roles use the same Supabase database, with role-specific dashboards and permissions. Account profiles and role assignments remain protected by their own table policies. The application uses Supabase Auth and a shared Supabase database. The Supabase service-role key is used only by the server-side Edge Functions and must never be entered into the browser application. The One Cainta application logo is loaded from the root-level `one-cainta-app-logo.png` file, which is also used to generate the PWA and Home Screen icons.

Sign in with email and password only; the app does not request an OTP or second-factor code. New registrations, invitation/recovery password setup, and password changes require at least eight characters with uppercase and lowercase letters, a number, and a special character. Configure Supabase Auth's password policy to match. With Supabase email confirmation enabled, the app displays a check-your-email screen after signup; after the user opens the confirmation link, the app verifies the saved profile and displays an Account Created screen with a Sign In button instead of opening the homepage or workspace. Confirmed self-registrations automatically receive the User role; no Admin approval is required. Run [`supabase/migrations/20261015000000_ensure_confirmed_signup_profiles.sql`](./supabase/migrations/20261015000000_ensure_confirmed_signup_profiles.sql) to ensure the auth triggers assign profiles at signup/confirmation and to repair already-confirmed accounts that are missing profiles. The confirmation screen can resend a fresh link, and the sign-in screen can also resend one for an unconfirmed account. Staff and Admin access require an authorized invitation or role assignment. Supabase Auth sessions persist in browser local storage, and workspace sessions warn after 10 minutes of inactivity before signing out after a further 60 seconds; selecting Continue or interacting with the workspace keeps the session active. Invitees set their password from the invitation link, then sign in with email and password.

Sign-in locks for one minute after five consecutive invalid email/password attempts for the same email in the same browser, and shows a live countdown. Failed attempts reset after 15 minutes without another failure; a successful password sign-in clears them. This browser-side lockout improves the user experience but is not a substitute for Supabase Auth's server-side rate limits.

Sign-in, registration, and password-recovery screens share the same authentication card styling and use a short staggered entrance animation with smooth card resizing when switching forms. The animation respects the browser's reduced-motion preference.

Workspace page transitions animate the dashboard content and controls, with support for reduced-motion preferences. The current workspace page is stored in the URL fragment (for example, `#participants`) so refreshing returns to that page when the same browser session is active.
On mobile browsers, workspace navigation opens as a slide-in drawer over a dimmed page; it starts closed and can be dismissed with the close button, backdrop, Escape key, or after selecting a page.
Installed apps check for shell updates when opened. Core scripts and styles are fetched from the live site when online, with the cached copy used when offline, so role-specific navigation updates do not remain stuck on an old install.
The **Install App** button is fixed at the bottom-right of the screen; status toasts appear above it so neither control obscures the other. Sign out from the account menu beside the profile avatar; the workspace navigation itself has no sign-out button.

The landing page, authentication screens, and workspace include a dark/light theme toggle with a zoom-reveal transition from the selected toggle. The selected theme is saved in the browser and restored before the page is displayed; the transition is disabled when reduced motion is requested or unsupported by the browser.

Select the workspace avatar to open **Profile** and **Sign out**. Profile settings let each signed-in account update its own full name and contact number, request an email change through Supabase confirmation, and change its password after verifying the current password. Run [`supabase/migrations/20261025000000_user_profile_contact_details.sql`](./supabase/migrations/20261025000000_user_profile_contact_details.sql) to add the contact-number field and grant users access only to their own name and contact details; the migration is additive and does not allow users to change their role.

The website can be installed as a Progressive Web App (PWA) without a Play Store listing. The floating **Install App** button remains visible on desktop and mobile browsers until the app is installed. On desktop, selecting it explains that installation is available only on a mobile device. On supported Android browsers, it opens the browser's install prompt; on iPhone and iPad, open the site in Safari and use **Share → Add to Home Screen**. Installation and service-worker caching require HTTPS (localhost is supported for development), and each device must first open the site online and sign in to save its workspace copy. Staff and Residents can enable phone push alerts from the notification bell; notifications require browser/device permission, an installed Home Screen app on iOS, and configured VAPID secrets. Browsers only allow the permission prompt after a user action, so the app cannot force permission or bypass a denial.

After that first successful online sign-in, a device can open the cached app and view its last saved workspace data offline. Admins can create, edit, and delete operational records offline; Staff can update assigned programs; Users can queue their own enrollment or cancellation. Changes are stored on that device and sync to Supabase when connectivity returns. If the same record was changed both offline and online, the app keeps both copies and asks the Admin, assigned Staff member, or affected User (for their own enrollment only) to choose which one to use. New sign-ins, role checks, account management/invitations, activity reporting, notifications, official external updates, profile changes, and cross-device synchronization require an internet connection. If a saved sign-in expires while offline, reconnect and sign in again to sync queued work. Offline workspace data is stored in the browser's local storage on that device; use a trusted, screen-locked device, especially when handling participant and emergency-contact details. The app's cached shell and saved records do not make Supabase-backed services or other websites work offline.

The five operational modules support creating, editing, searching, and deleting records where appropriate. Training programs have a separate disaster-type category and filter for quickly finding Flood, Earthquake, Fire, Typhoon, and other training. When adding or editing a program, Admins can type a venue or pin it on the Cainta-centered OpenStreetMap; a selected pin is saved with the program. Admins assign each instructor to the programs they manage. The dashboard summarizes upcoming training and readiness scores. Drill reports can be printed or exported as CSV.

## Set up Supabase

1. Create a Supabase project and set its URL and **publishable anon key** (or legacy anon key) in `DEFAULT_CONFIG` in `app.js`. The publishable key is intended for browser use when Row Level Security is enabled; never expose the secret/service-role key or database password.
2. In the Supabase SQL Editor, run [`supabase/migrations/20261014000000_readyph_admin_roles.sql`](./supabase/migrations/20261014000000_readyph_admin_roles.sql), [`supabase/migrations/20261014010000_read_only_user_role.sql`](./supabase/migrations/20261014010000_read_only_user_role.sql), [`supabase/migrations/20261014020000_auto_approve_confirmed_users.sql`](./supabase/migrations/20261014020000_auto_approve_confirmed_users.sql), and [`supabase/migrations/20261015000000_ensure_confirmed_signup_profiles.sql`](./supabase/migrations/20261015000000_ensure_confirmed_signup_profiles.sql). Then run [`supabase/migrations/20261014060000_disable_email_otp.sql`](./supabase/migrations/20261014060000_disable_email_otp.sql) to restore direct role-based RLS access if the earlier OTP migrations (300–500) were applied. The 600 migration removes the OTP-only challenge table and restores role-based access; it does not delete accounts or workspace records. For a fresh setup, skip the legacy OTP migrations 300–500.
   If the first two migrations have already been applied, run the full third migration to enable automatic User role assignment. If you previously ran an earlier version of the third migration, rerun it after replacing the old SQL with the current file contents. Existing projects that already ran migration 500 should apply migration 600.
   Run [`supabase/migrations/20261021000000_account_activity_management.sql`](./supabase/migrations/20261021000000_account_activity_management.sql) after the role-access migrations to add account display status and secure last-seen tracking. If the Workspace accounts page reports that `user_profiles.account_status` does not exist, run this migration in the Supabase SQL Editor; it is safe to rerun if some of its changes are already present.
   Run [`supabase/migrations/20261025000000_user_profile_contact_details.sql`](./supabase/migrations/20261025000000_user_profile_contact_details.sql) to enable personal contact details in Profile settings.
   For a new or existing installation, run [`supabase/migrations/20261027000000_user_staff_admin_roles.sql`](./supabase/migrations/20261027000000_user_staff_admin_roles.sql), then the additive Admin, Staff, Resident, attendance-gated evaluation, and notification migrations in timestamp order (`20261027130000` through `20261027180000`). These migrations add role modules, Staff field operations, feedback, resident household records, attendance-gated evaluations, role-scoped notifications, and push-subscription storage. Apply all migrations before publishing the updated app or Edge Functions.
   The production account assignments for `caintaone24@gmail.com` (Admin) and `youtacharlie@gmail.com` (User) are recorded in [`supabase/migrations/20261027120000_assign_requested_account_roles.sql`](./supabase/migrations/20261027120000_assign_requested_account_roles.sql). Apply this account-specific migration only when both accounts exist in the linked project.
3. In **Authentication → Users**, create the first trusted account from the dashboard. The database trigger creates its profile; if its email is confirmed, it may initially receive the User role. In the SQL Editor, promote that specific account to Admin:

   ```sql
   update public.user_profiles
   set role = 'admin'
   where lower(email) = lower('your-admin-email@example.com');
   ```

   Confirm that exactly one profile was updated. Enable email sign-ups in **Authentication → Sign In / Providers → Email**. Use Supabase's built-in `{{ .ConfirmationURL }}` link in the **Confirm signup** and **Reset Password** email templates. If using a custom token-hash link instead, use `<a href="{{ .RedirectTo }}?token_hash={{ .TokenHash }}&amp;type=signup">Confirm your email</a>` for signup and `<a href="{{ .RedirectTo }}?token_hash={{ .TokenHash }}&amp;type=recovery">Reset password</a>` for recovery. The app redeems these token-hash links directly and handles the standard Supabase confirmation links. Do not replace the link with a one-time code. Configure Supabase CAPTCHA and Auth rate limits to reduce automated sign-up abuse. If the site should be invite-only, keep public sign-ups disabled; Admin invitations will still work.
4. Set the Auth **Site URL** to `https://polite-salamander-82ba90.netlify.app` and allow that exact URL in **Redirect URLs**. The production project currently allows the site root only; both signup confirmation and password recovery must redirect there. The app handles Supabase's signup and recovery callback parameters at the root, and password recovery opens the in-app new-password form. Do not set email redirects to `/account-created` unless that exact URL is added to the Supabase allow list. Never use localhost or `127.0.0.1` for links that will be opened on another device. Update the Auth Site URL and allowed Redirect URLs when changing to a custom domain. Configure trusted custom SMTP in Supabase Auth for reliable signup confirmation and invite/reset email delivery.
5. To enable **Continue with Google**, create a Web OAuth client in Google Cloud Console. Add the live site URL as an authorized JavaScript origin and `https://YOUR_PROJECT_REF.supabase.co/auth/v1/callback` as an authorized redirect URI. In Supabase, open **Authentication → Sign In / Providers → Google**, enable Google, and enter that client's ID and secret. The app's Google button returns users to `AUTH_REDIRECT_URL`. Google sign-in requires this provider setup; email-and-password sign-in works independently.
6. Deploy the account-management and training-notification Edge Functions. Install/use the Supabase CLI, authenticate, and link this project:

   ```powershell
   npx supabase login
   npx supabase link --project-ref YOUR_PROJECT_REF
   npx supabase functions deploy invite-admin
   npx supabase functions deploy delete-account
   npx supabase functions deploy notify-new-training
   npx supabase functions deploy workspace-push
   npx supabase secrets set APP_ORIGIN=http://127.0.0.1:5500
   ```

   Change `APP_ORIGIN` to the final HTTPS site URL for production and redeploy the functions after changing it. To enable phone push, generate a VAPID key pair in a trusted environment and set `VAPID_PUBLIC_KEY`, `VAPID_PRIVATE_KEY`, and `VAPID_SUBJECT` as Supabase secrets. Keep the private key server-side; never put it in `app.js`, HTML, or browser storage. Push delivery is sent for new or updated training programs and simulation scenarios. To enable Gmail announcements, configure `GMAIL_CLIENT_ID`, `GMAIL_CLIENT_SECRET`, `GMAIL_REFRESH_TOKEN`, and `GMAIL_SENDER` as Supabase Edge Function secrets. The Google OAuth grant must include `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.send`, and the Gmail API must be enabled for the associated Google Cloud project. The Admin dashboard reports email-delivery failures and allows retries; SMS alerts are not connected. Edge Functions use Supabase-provided server environment variables for service-role access; never put those values in browser code. The `email-mfa` function and its Resend secrets are not needed for the current email-and-password sign-in.
7. Serve this folder over HTTP during development; Supabase Auth does not work reliably from a `file://` URL:

   ```powershell
   python -m http.server 8080 --bind 127.0.0.1
   ```

   With VS Code Live Server, open `http://127.0.0.1:5500` (or `http://localhost:5500`). Do not open the app using a `file://` URL. `APP_ORIGIN` is used by the invitation Edge Function and should match the origin where invitees will use the site. Signup confirmation and password-reset links use `AUTH_REDIRECT_URL` in `app.js` so email links open on the hosted HTTPS site. Supabase Auth sessions are kept in this browser.

## Account and instructor workflow

1. An Admin opens **Account Management → Invite account**, enters a name and email, and chooses **User** (community portal), **Staff** (instructor), or **Admin**.
2. The invited person follows the Supabase invitation email link, sets a password, then signs in with that password.
3. In **Account Management**, an Admin may select User, Staff, Admin, or Pending, then use the row's **Save** button and confirm with their current Admin password. Admins can search and filter accounts and change display status. Other Admin accounts must first be demoted before deletion; the signed-in account cannot be deleted or change its own role, and the database prevents demoting the final Admin. Role changes take effect in database policies immediately; an affected user must sign in again to refresh the interface. Deploy the updated `invite-admin` and `delete-account` Edge Functions before publishing the updated account-management UI.
4. To assign an instructor, an Admin creates or edits a training program and selects a Staff account in **Assigned instructor**. Each program also has a separate **Disaster type** category and filter. Staff can view and update assigned programs only; Users have their own sessions, performance, drill browsing, profile, and notification pages, but cannot change shared programs or records. Admins manage the other operational modules.

If the last Admin account is lost, a trusted project owner must restore access using the Supabase SQL Editor. Do not grant a self-registered account elevated access as a recovery shortcut.

## Security and data handling

Participant and emergency-contact details are personal information. Limit administrator access to trusted staff, use HTTPS, configure backups and retention, and follow applicable privacy requirements. Invitation delivery depends on Supabase Auth email/SMTP configuration. The static frontend is not a replacement for a reviewed production deployment and operational security policies.
